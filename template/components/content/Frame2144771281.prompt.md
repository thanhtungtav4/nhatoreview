```jsx
<ArticleCompact image="…/thumb.png" title="Thiết Kế Biệt Thự Art Deco" chip="Tin mới" />

```

`Frame2144771281` (Frame 2144771281) is the 880-wide list row with an excerpt. `ArticleCompact` (Frame 2144771605) is the 388-wide sidebar rail item with a 94px square thumb and an optional gold chip. `ThumbCard` (Frame 1731) is the 280×268 stacked card with no meta at all. All three load the icon sprite from `assets/icons/nhato-icons.js`.
